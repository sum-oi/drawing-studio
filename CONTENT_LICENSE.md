# Course and illustration licensing

Original lesson text, curriculum descriptions, and geometric teaching diagrams
included in this repository are distributed under the same MIT license as the code.

Artist names and references identify study directions only. This project does not
include those artists' images, brush packs, paid lessons, or licenses to their work.
The project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the referenced artists.

User-uploaded artwork and private practice records remain the user's content.
They are not included in this repository, image, or project license.

The botanical studio mark and still-life SVGs are original project artwork,
distributed under MIT. The editorial palette and paper-like treatment were
informed by a user-supplied How Many Plants style reference; no source artwork
or proprietary fonts are bundled.

Interface refinement used [Unslop by Damir Sokolovsky (@heydamir)](https://heydamir.design/unslop),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Its design guidance was adapted to a Chinese drawing practice interface.
